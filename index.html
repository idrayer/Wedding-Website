Our Story

How We Got Here

Maggie and Ian met through a twist of fate. Ian signed up for a softball league as a free agent, but got assigned to Maggie's pickleball team instead.

From there, Thursday night pickleball turned into weekend Renaissance Faire, Mardi Gras, rodeos, regular taco nights, sleeping through Game of Thrones and more; all before the couple realized they were dating.

Their love grew over time with nightly walks of Freckles, Maggie's adorable dalmation mix (according to her papers) that's always in search of a thunder buddy and snackos.

Now, they enjoy traveling together ziplining through jungles, hiking to waterfalls, swimming with dolphins and trying new local experiences. Somehow, Maggie has learned to put up with Ian's gratuitous puns and terrible jokes.

They look forward to welcoming you to their wedding Memorial Day weekend, 2027 in Central Texas Hill Country.

Where to Stay

Nearby Accommodations

The Waters Point is located a short walk from the town square of Wimberley, a town in the hill country of Central Texas

Wimberley is home to a number of small inns and cottages such as Wimberley Inn, Wimberley Square Inn, 7A Ranch, and Cabins on the Square.

Wimberley is also nearby to San Marcos, Kyle, and Dripping springs, which have a number of hotels and motels from major chains like Fairfield by Marriott Inn & Suites San Marcos, Hampton Inn Kyle, Courtyard by Marriott Austin Dripping Springs. No room blocks are being made for these hotels.

Things to Do

Wimberley & Hill Country Favorites

If you're making a weekend of it, here are a few easy ways to explore Wimberley and the surrounding Hill Country.

Jacob's Well

A classic Hill Country stop for a scenic swim, short hike, and one of the area's most famous springs.

Blue Hole Regional Park

Relax under the cypress trees, walk the trails, or cool off at a picture-perfect Texas swimming hole.

Wine Tours

Spend an afternoon tasting your way through the Hill Country's wineries, vineyards, and scenic back roads.

Schlitterbahn Waterpark

A fun day trip for anyone who wants waterslides, lazy rivers, and a little extra adventure nearby.

Explore Downtown Wimberley

Wander the square for boutiques, galleries, coffee, and Hill Country restaurants in a walkable small-town setting.

Fredericksburg Elephant Sanctuary

Make a memorable Hill Country day trip to see rescued elephants and learn more about the sanctuary's conservation-focused mission.
